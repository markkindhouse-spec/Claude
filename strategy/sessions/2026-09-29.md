## Round 2 — live vendor landscape pull

**Where it happened:** Claude Code, background research pulled live during
a text sparring session.

Findings (verify before acting, this is a fast-moving space):

- Full unmanned autonomy for civil earthmoving is now commercially live,
  not hypothetical. Bedrock Robotics (new, well-funded, ex-Waymo team) has
  paid, no-operator-in-cab excavation deployments running in 2026 on real
  jobs. Scope is narrow so far: rough grading and mass excavation only,
  not fine grade or trenching.
- Teleoperation (remote human control, no autonomy claim) is the mature,
  proven option. Caterpillar Command has been doing this at scale for
  years; Cat claims one operator can run 4-5 machines remotely.
- Built Robotics (retrofit kit vendor) has a training partnership with
  the operating engineers union (IUOE) — relevant if the fleet is a union
  shop, since it's a cooperative framing rather than a threat to the
  bargaining unit.
- Real open gap: no CA-specific regulation found for autonomous
  equipment; insurance/bonding treatment is case-by-case per carrier.
  Needs a direct call to the surety and GL carrier before this goes near
  a public bid, not after.

Framing question raised, not yet answered: is the bet the boring proven
thing (teleop, Cat Command, near-term utilization gain) or the new risky
thing (full-autonomy pilot, Bedrock, becomes part of the platform story
told to BDX)? Mark's answer: doesn't know yet, that's what the brainstorm
is for. Picking this back up next session.
